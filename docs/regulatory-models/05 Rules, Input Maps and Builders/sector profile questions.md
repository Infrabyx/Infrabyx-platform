# Profile questions for the sector Applicability Engines (APP-M02 to APP-M07)

The same questions are asked in the section "Questions that settle conditional obligations" in Part A of document 1 (Entity Profile and Engagement Preferences) of each sector pack and on the Profile sheet of the engine. Each is answered Yes, No or In part. A "Conditional" line of the sector Policy Register settled by a question takes a Yes or No answer; "In part" leaves its lines to be answered one by one on the Policies sheet. A line-level answer on the Policies sheet always takes precedence.
