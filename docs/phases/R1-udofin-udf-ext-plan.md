## Mock lender (`packages/fakes/src/lender.ts`)

Offers carry `monthly_instalment_paise` and `penal_charges_text`, a sanctioned offer a `kfs_url`; `offer_ref` must name an offer the lender made (`offer_unknown`), and the chosen offer, or else the request, sets amount and tenor; status changes and a rejection carry the lender's words; the KFS carries the full detail (a monthly schedule, a stamp-duty charge, cooling-off days, exit terms, the nodal officer and the LSP entry); the sanction letter its `document_ref`; `LoanDisbursed` the application id and time; an acceptance naming another KFS version, hash or application is refused (`kfs_mismatch`, `application_mismatch`); the first exit request in the cooling-off period is answered once with `CoolingOffExitAnswered` (principal plus the APR for the days held, at least one day).

3. `household.monthly_household_income_paise` and `is_microfinance` sit inside `household`, so they need its `household_ref`.
4. `economic_profile.age_years` is 18 to 120.
5. The Annex shapes (`co_lending`, `lsp_recovery_agent`, `nodal_gro`) and the KFS charge fields are [VERIFY] against the RBI KFS format; `co_lending` shares must add up to 100 %.
