V-CIP webhooks verified without a vendor call (Udofin PR-UDF-046; R1-D-199). `KycPort.verifyWebhook(rawBody, headers, meta?) → { eventId, vcipId, receivedAt }` (`KycWebhookReceipt`), optional on the interface, checks a webhook's size, signature, window and replay only, with no vendor call and no read-back, so a product answers 401 to an unverified event, records `eventId` in its module inbox, acknowledges within 2 s and reads the session back with `vcipStatus` from its queue (IPB-10-27). `handleWebhook` keeps its signature and, where `verifyWebhook` exists, is `verifyWebhook` and then `vcipStatus`. `contracts` adds `verifyKycWebhookWith` (the order: the body's size, at most `KYC_WEBHOOK_MAX_BODY_BYTES` (64 KiB) before anything is read; the Standard Webhooks headers; the signature and `KYC_WEBHOOK_TOLERANCE_SECONDS` (300) window; the replay claim on the delivery's id and timestamp; then the session id), `KYC_WEBHOOK_REFUSALS` (`body_too_large`, `signature_invalid`, `timestamp_stale`, `event_replayed`; `IPB-API-018`, definite), `kycWebhookRefused`, `KYC_WEBHOOK_MALFORMED` (`IPB-PORT-001` for a verified event naming no session) and `KycWebhookReplayStore`. `crypto` adds `verifyStandardWebhook` (C-13: HMAC-SHA-256 over `<id>.<timestamp>.<body>` as IPB-10-21 defines it, constant time, ±5 minutes, `whsec_` secrets, rotation, `v1` only) and `signStandardWebhookForTest`; no new algorithm. `FakeKyc` verifies Standard Webhooks (`FAKE_KYC_WEBHOOK`, `signFakeKycWebhook`, option `webhook: { secrets, replay }`, `vcipStatusVendorCalls`), and its `handleWebhook` now refuses an unsigned event. `KycFixtures.webhook` (optional) drives two new contract cases. `connectors` adds `standardWebhookKycVerifier` for an adapter whose provider signs with Standard Webhooks; `DigioKyc` and `IdfyKyc` refuse `verifyWebhook` `unsupported`; `kycMethodRouter` routes it under `vcip`.
