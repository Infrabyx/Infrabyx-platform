`PartnerMtlsClient` (`@infrabyx/connectors/egress`; Udofin PR-UDF-069; R1-D-250): one outbound call to a configured partner over mutual TLS. Built with the partner's exact `hosts`, `clientCertificateRef` and `clientKeyRef` (SECRETS references, resolved with `resolveSecret`, which `secretsResolver` from `@infrabyx/config` fits), an optional `serverCa` (otherwise Node's bundled roots) and limits. `request({ method, url, body, idempotencyKey, contentType?, accept?, headers?, signal?, timeoutMs?, maxResponseBytes? })`: `POST` or `PUT` only, a non-empty body (1 MiB by default), a required `Idempotency-Key` (`IPB-API-010` when missing or ill-formed), https on port 443 to one of the hosts with no user-info or IP literal; DNS once, every answer public, the socket pinned to the checked address, SNI and the server certificate verified against the host name, TLS 1.2 or later; a 3xx is refused (`redirect`), never followed; the answer is capped (1 MiB by default, `IPB-SEC-013`) and must be identity-encoded; one deadline (15 s by default) and the caller's `AbortSignal` (`IPB-PORT-002`, `timeout` or `aborted`, with `meta.maybeSent`). The certificate and key are checked with `checkClientCredential` and kept only in a TLS secure context for `credentialTtlMs` (5 minutes by default, never past notAfter); `checkCredentials()` resolves them without calling the partner and answers the dates and `x5tS256`; `forgetCredentials()` drops the cache. Refusals are `IPB-SEC-018`, `IPB-PORT-001`, `IPB-PORT-002` or `IPB-PORT-005` with a fixed `meta.reason`, never the URL's path or query, a header value, the body or the key. Only P-256 client keys and SECRETS-held keys (not KMS-held) for now (owner question #180). The public-address check, host-name rule and DNS pin of the `governed` adapter move unchanged to `egress/guard.ts` and are exported (`allAddressesPublic`, `checkPublicHostName`, `pinnedLookup`); `isPublicAddress` is exported as before. No setting or migration.
