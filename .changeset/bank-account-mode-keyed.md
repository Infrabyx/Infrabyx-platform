Bank account checks by mode, keyed retries and the penny-drop funding statement (Udofin PR-UDF-043; R1-D-196). `BankAccountCheck` gains an optional `mode` (`"pennyless"` or `"penny_drop"`, `BANK_ACCOUNT_MODES`) and `BankAccountResult` an optional `mode` (the mode used; always set by the platform's adapters and the fake). An adapter that cannot honour the mode asked refuses `IPB-PORT-002` with `meta.reason: "mode_unsupported"` (`BANK_ACCOUNT_MODE_UNSUPPORTED`) before any vendor call, never falling back to another mode; omitted, the adapter's configured mode is used and reported. `bankAccountVerify` is idempotent on `call.idempotencyKey` for 24 hours: a retry gets the first answer with no vendor call, and the same key with another account, IFSC, name or mode is `IPB-PORT-004`; a keyed penny drop whose first call did not finish is never sent again (`pending_unresolved`). The status set is unchanged (`BANK_ACCOUNT_STATUSES`). `contracts` adds `KYC_ADAPTER_FUNDING` and `kycAdapterFunding(adapter)`: per adapter, who funds a penny drop (`provider`, `tenant` or `unknown`) and the source, a statement only; Digio and IDfy are `unknown` until verified. `DigioKyc` and `IdfyKyc` now provide pennyless bank account checks (stub-shaped, adapters off) and refuse a penny drop `mode_unsupported`; `connectors` adds `BankAccountVerifyStore`, `PgBankAccountVerifyStore`, `MemoryBankAccountVerifyStore`, `keyedBankAccountVerify` and the migration `0012_kyc_bank_account_verify.sql` (`connectors.kyc_bank_account_verify`, forced RLS); `kycMethodRouter` moves a check past an adapter that refuses `mode_unsupported`, but not a keyed check or a penny drop past an unavailable one; the router's breaker no longer counts `mode_unsupported`. `FakeKyc` honours both modes (options `bankAccountModes`, `bankAccountMode`) and counts `bankAccountVendorCalls`; `fakes` adds `KycFixtures.bankAccount` (`BankAccountFixtures`); `testing` adds four contract cases and the Digio and IDfy stubs' bank accounts.
