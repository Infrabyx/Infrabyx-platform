A customer step-up for sensitive borrower actions (Udofin PR-UDF-056; R1-D-244). `CustomerIdentityBase.startStepUp` and `confirmStepUp` (Keycloak and Zitadel customer adapters): a fresh one-time code to the account's registered login, made by the platform (salted SHA-256 only), sent through NOTIFY, bound to the key of the active device session and guarded like a sign-in code; on success the device's `step_up_at` and `step_up_acr` are recorded and ledgered (`identity.customer.device.step_up`). `verifyRequest` answers `acr` (`otp`) and `auth_time` (the later of the device's sign-in and its last step-up) and takes `fresh: true` to read the device past the 15 s cache. New `assertCustomerStepUp(claims, policy, now?)` (refuses with `IPB-SEC-001` in the step-up form: `meta.step_up`, `customer`, `required_acr`, `max_age`, nothing else) and `customerStepUpChallenge(e)` (the RFC 9470 401 with `WWW-Authenticate: DPoP error="insufficient_user_authentication", acr_values=..., max_age=...`); `stepUpChallenge` ignores a customer refusal. New optional `CustomerAuthStore.recordStepUp` (`PgCustomerAuthStore`, `MemoryCustomerAuthStore`), `activeDevice(..., { fresh })`, `DeviceRecord.stepUpAt`/`stepUpAcr`, `CUSTOMER_STEP_UP_ACTION`, `CUSTOMER_AUTH_TIME_SKEW_SECONDS`. Migration `0017_customer_device_step_up` (expand-only: two nullable columns on `connectors.customer_device`); apply it before upgrading. No IdP or Keycloak extension change.
