Execution-pack evidence additions (Udofin PR-UDF-075; R1-D-258), all optional, so every pack valid before still parses. (1) `estamp_system` (`ESTAMP_SYSTEMS`: `SHCIL`, `STATE_PORTAL`, `NESL_DDE`, `GRAS`) on `PackStampEvidence` (only with `channel: E_STAMP`) and on the manifest's `StampEvidence` (only with `mode: ESTAMP`). (2) `reason_code` (`NOT_STAMPABLE_REASON_CODES`: `NOT_AN_INSTRUMENT`, `ACKNOWLEDGEMENT`, `OTHER`) on `ExecutionPackIssuedData.not_stampable[]`, and `not_stampable_reason_code` on a manifest item (only with `not_stampable: true`); the free-text reason stays required for every code. (3) `disbursement_clause.page` (1 to 2000) and `clause_ref` (`4.2`, `4.2(a)`) in both carriers, beside the still-required `page_or_clause_ref`, whose read forms (`p.3`, `cl.4.2`, `p.3 cl.4.2`, …) are stated and parsed by the new `parsePageOrClauseRef`; a `page` or `clause_ref` that names another page or clause than a readable `page_or_clause_ref` is refused. (4) The rule for `PackStampEvidence.certificate_document_id` is stated: a document the lender shared for the same application, not necessarily listed in the pack; the schema still refuses only the stamped document naming itself.
