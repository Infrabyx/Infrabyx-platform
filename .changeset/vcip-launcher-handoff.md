The RE-hosted V-CIP launcher and its signed hand-off (Udofin PR-UDF-045; R1-D-198). `KycPort.launchVcip({ context, jws }, call) → { vcipId, launchUrl, expiresAt }` (`VcipLaunch`, with the evidence), optional on the interface, lets an LSP hand a customer to an RE's V-CIP without hosting any V-CIP component. `contracts` adds `VcipHandoffContext` (tenant, RE, LSP, customer reference, journey id, issued-at, expires-at, nonce: ids and times only, strict, at most `VCIP_HANDOFF_MAX_LIFETIME_SECONDS` (900) from issue to expiry, no field with a 12-digit run or an Aadhaar-like number), `LaunchVcip`, `VCIP_HANDOFF_REFUSALS` (`handoff_invalid`, `handoff_replayed`, `handoff_expired`), `vcipHandoffFailure`, `vcipHandoffTimeRefusal`, `vcipLaunchUrlAllowed` (`https:`, the adapter's exact hosts, no user information, no number) and `VCIP_LAUNCH_URL_REFUSED`. The LSP signs the context with the existing `signRegulatedMessage`; the adapter verifies it with `verifyRegulatedMessage` and its replay store before any vendor call (nothing new in cryptography), and refuses `IPB-PORT-001` with a fixed label, definite. `vcipStatus(vcipId, call?)` takes an optional `PortCall` and reads under its tenant. `VcipManifest` gains `reasonCode` (`VCIP_REASON_CODES`) and `liveness.mode` (`LIVENESS_MODES`); the free-text `reason` stays, deprecated, never to be shown. `VcipSession` gains an optional `vcipId` to start a launched session. `FakeKyc` implements the launcher (`FAKE_VCIP_HANDOFF`, `signFakeVcipHandoff`, `fakeVcipHandoffKeys`, `fakeVcipHandoffContext`, `vcipLaunchVendorCalls`); `KycFixtures.vcipLaunch` (optional) drives three new contract cases. `VcipConductor` takes hand-offs (`handoff: { reId, issuers, replay, launchBaseUrl, launchHosts? }`), schedules the session the RE's official starts with `startVcip({ …, vcipId })`, expires it with its hand-off (`launch_expired`), takes a `reasonCode` on `complete` and `drop`, a liveness `mode` on `assess`, and a `PortCall` or tenant on `vcipStatus`. `DigioKyc` and `IdfyKyc` refuse `launchVcip` `unsupported`; `kycMethodRouter` routes it under `vcip` and never moves a launch past an unavailable adapter.
