Tenant SSO brokers on `KeycloakIdentity` and `ZitadelIdentity` (PR-LDC-09; R1-D-78), linking only on both: a brokered login reaches only an account the product provisioned in the broker's tenant, and never creates one. Keycloak: realm identity providers built from Keycloak's own `import-config` of the discovery or metadata URL, PKCE S256, signatures validated, `trustEmail` off, owner tenant, verified domains and source URL in `KEYCLOAK_BROKER_KEYS`, and the first-login flow `ipb-broker-link-only` (`KEYCLOAK_BROKER_LINK_FLOW`: detect the existing account, then re-authentication; no account creation, no profile review), which `applyRealm` creates and restores with the new `identityBrokers` realm option, with an `idp` access-token claim and the admin grant `manage-identity-providers`; a session whose broker belongs to another tenant than its account is refused (`IPB-IDN-001` `idp_tenant`). Zitadel: organisation generic OIDC or SAML IdPs with linking only (no account creation), on the login policy while enabled, routed by organisation metadata `ipb.idp.<alias>`; a user's token is refused while the account is linked to another tenant's broker (`idp_tenant`). Both: the client secret resolved through the new `resolveSecret` option (a SECRETS resolver) and never returned; an alias owned by another tenant or configured by hand is refused (`IPB-PORT-004` `alias_taken`), as is a domain already routed (`domain_taken`); discovery maps a verified email domain to its enabled broker (capability `discoverIdentityProvider: emulated`). `applyRealm` takes a `fetchImpl` test option.
