`@infrabyx/connectors/kyc`: `DigioKyc` (`digio-kyc`), the KYC reference adapter for DigiLocker and Aadhaar Offline e-KYC (owner answer #87, R1-D-24). It is off behind `int.kyc.digio.enabled` and `unproven` (#147) until the owner's Digio account exists. Every other KYC method, V-CIP included (#90), is `unsupported`.
