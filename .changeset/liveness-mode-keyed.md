Liveness by mode, applied thresholds, keyed retries and the template retention statement (Udofin PR-UDF-044; R1-D-197). `LivenessCheck` gains an optional `mode` (`"passive"`, `"active_gesture"` or `"active_voice"`, `LIVENESS_MODES`) and `ScoreResult` an optional `threshold` (the threshold the adapter applied, for liveness and face match) and `mode` (the liveness mode used); the platform's adapters and the fake always set them. An adapter that cannot honour the mode refuses `IPB-PORT-002` with `meta.reason: "mode_unsupported"` (`KYC_MODE_UNSUPPORTED`, which `BANK_ACCOUNT_MODE_UNSUPPORTED` now equals) before any vendor call, never falling back; omitted, the configured mode is used and reported. `livenessCheck` and `faceMatch` are idempotent on `call.idempotencyKey` for 24 hours, a key scoped to its method: a retry gets the first answer with no vendor call, and the same key with other frames, another mode or other images is `IPB-PORT-004`. `score` is a finite number from 0 to 1 (`kycScoreValid`): an adapter normalises a vendor score on its known scale and refuses any other `IPB-PORT-003` with `meta.reason: "score_out_of_range"` (`KYC_SCORE_OUT_OF_RANGE`), never clamping. `contracts` adds `KYC_ADAPTER_RETENTION` and `kycAdapterRetention(adapter)`: per adapter, whether the vendor keeps a face template or embedding beyond the call (`none`, `kept` or `unknown`) and the source, a statement only; Digio and IDfy are `unknown` until a written vendor statement. `IdfyKyc` honours `passive` liveness only (its photo liveness), reports its thresholds (now checked to be from 0 to 1), reads scores on the scale `IDFY_SCORE_SCALE` (1, or the option `scoreScale: 100`) and refuses one off it instead of guessing the scale and clamping; `DigioKyc` has no liveness or face-match path and still refuses both `unsupported`. `connectors` adds `KycScoreCheckStore`, `PgKycScoreCheckStore`, `MemoryKycScoreCheckStore`, `keyedScoreCheck`, `livenessMode`, `scoreOnScale` and the migration `0013_kyc_score_check.sql` (`connectors.kyc_score_check`, forced RLS; no image, template, embedding or storage reference in clear); `kycMethodRouter` moves any call past `mode_unsupported` and keeps a keyed liveness check or face match with an unavailable adapter. `FakeKyc` honours every liveness mode (options `livenessModes`, `livenessMode`, `livenessThreshold`, `faceMatchThreshold`), counts `livenessVendorCalls` and `faceMatchVendorCalls`, and `offScaleNext()` makes its next score off the scale; `fakes` adds `KycFixtures.scores` (`ScoreFixtures`); `testing` adds seven contract cases and the IDfy stub's `livenessChecks`, `faceMatches` and `scoreOnce`.
