`FakeEsign` as an ESP with certificates, STORAGE and multi-document envelopes (Udofin PR-UDF-070; R1-D-251). Every signature record carries `signerCertificate` from a throwaway test CA (made on first use; the common name is the signer's name). `new FakeEsign({ storage, now? })` reads each document from STORAGE (refusing `hash_mismatch` or a non-PDF), signs it as a real PAdES incremental update per signer, and on the first download writes the signed PDF and each signer's PEM chain to STORAGE (REGULATED, IPB-01-27 keys under the document's tenant, SHA-256 of the bytes; `certChainRef` is the chain's key). `createMultiDocumentEnvelope`, `downloadSignedDocuments`, `signDocument` and the envelope's `documents` status: it completes only when every document is signed by every signer. Test hooks: `signedObjects()`, `failNextDownload(error?)`, `setSignerCertificate(ref, testSignerOrDerOrPem)`, `decline(id, ref)`, `trustStore()`. `new FakeEsign()` and `new FakeEsign(() => date)` keep the earlier download reference; signing again for a signer who already signed is now a no-op, and the not-completed refusal carries `meta.reason: "not_completed"`.
