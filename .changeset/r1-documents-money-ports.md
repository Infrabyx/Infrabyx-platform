The `ESTAMP`, `PAYOUTS`, `DOCGEN` and `PDF` port interfaces and the PAYMENTS mandate lifecycle (Finbyx PR-FBX-29; IPB-01; R1-D-52), interfaces, fakes and contract cases only. `EstampPort` (`covers`, `quoteDuty`, `purchase`, `attach`, `verify`, `handleWebhook`) with NeSL Digital Document Execution as optional methods (`ddeRegister`, `ddeStatus`, `ddeDownloadExecuted`); `PayoutsPort` (`createBeneficiary`, `validateAccount` with the holder's name, `payout`, `getStatus` with the UTR and value date, `handleWebhook`, `balance`; "accepted" is never final); `DocgenPort` (`render`, `renderBatch`, `renderHtml` to PDF or PDF/A, `previewHtml`, `listTemplates`, `validateTemplate`) and `PdfPort` (IPB-01's operations over STORAGE references); `MandateLifecycle` (`registerMandate` for eNACH, UPI AutoPay and physical NACH with the UMRN, `mandateStatus`, `cancelMandate`), optional on `PaymentsPort`. Every money-moving call carries an idempotency key, an amount in integer paise and a `MoneyAuthorisation` (an approved request and a user or service initiator); `assertMoneyCall` refuses an agent (`IPB-SEC-004`) and a missing approval or key (`IPB-UX-004`). `SignatureRecord` gains the optional `espTransactionId` and `signerIp`. `@infrabyx/testing`: `FakeEstamp`, `FakePayouts`, `FakeMandates`, `FakeDocgen`, `FakePdf`, `FakeEsign` and their contract cases. No vendor adapter: payout and mandate adapters wait for the owner (money movement).
