A verify-only PAdES/CMS signature verifier (Udofin PR-UDF-059; owner answer #162, C-28; R1-D-246). `verifyPadesSignatures(pdf, { trust?, revocation?, now?, maxBytes? })` checks every CAdES-detached or PKCS#7-detached signature embedded in a PDF: the ByteRange covers the file except `/Contents` (an incremental update after the signature is its own outcome), the CMS SignedData parses, `messageDigest` equals the ByteRange digest, the signature over the signed attributes verifies with the signer certificate, the chain reaches a pinned CCA India root through pinned intermediates, every certificate is valid at the signing time (a verified B-T timestamp, the signed `signingTime`, `/M`, or the verification time), key usage allows signing, and revocation is `good` or `revoked` only from CRLs or OCSP responses the caller passes (`not_checked` otherwise; nothing is fetched). RSA PKCS#1 v1.5 and PSS with SHA-256/384/512, ECDSA P-256/SHA-256 and P-384/SHA-384; SHA-1 and MD5 are `weak_algorithm`. Results are fixed labels (`PADES_OUTCOMES`, `PADES_DOCUMENT_OUTCOMES`) and never document text. The package's own strict DER reader is used; no new dependency. The crypto registry gains `sha-384`, `sha-512`, `rsassa-pkcs1-v1_5-sha384`/`-sha512` and `rsassa-pss-sha384`/`-sha512`, each for this verification only, and existing entries a C-28 use line. **`CCA_INDIA_TRUST` has no pinned certificate yet** (owner question #178), so every chain is `chain_untrusted` until a PR adds CCA's published root and intermediates. Tests only: `@infrabyx/crypto/pades-test`, a throwaway CA that signs PDFs and makes timestamps, CRLs and OCSP responses, and `customTrustStore` (results then say `trustStore: "custom"`). No signing, setting, migration or existing behaviour changes.
