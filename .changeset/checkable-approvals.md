The approvals a signed-in checker may check, paged (Udofin PR-UDF-047; R1-D-200). `ApprovalsEngine.listCheckable(ctx, { cursor?, limit? }) → { items: CheckableApproval[], nextCursor }` lists the tenant's pending, unexpired requests that the principal may decide now: never their own (maker ≠ checker), never one they have decided, and only where `decide`'s own rule lets them (the action's checker roles after the tenant's `mc.checker_roles` narrowing and its checker functions, and for a levelled request the open level's roles). An agent gets an empty list. `CheckableApproval` carries `id`, `action`, `summary`, `riskClass`, `makerId`, `createdAt`, `expiresAt`, `currentLevel`, `requiredCheckers` and `approvals`; never the payload, its digest or data class, or the diff. Oldest first, keyset pages on `(created_at, id)`, `limit` 1 to `CHECKABLE_MAX_LIMIT` (100, default `CHECKABLE_DEFAULT_LIMIT` 25), an opaque base64url `nextCursor`; a bad cursor or limit is refused with a `RangeError` before any read. Read through the kernel, so RLS keeps it to the tenant. `decide` now takes its checker rule from the same private function the list uses; who may check, the levels and every decision path are unchanged. Migration `tenancy/0025` adds a partial keyset index on pending, unexpired requests, built concurrently (run `infrabyx-migrate`; no column, policy or grant changes, export map unchanged).
